STEEL / FABRICATION DOCUMENTS

From the FEM model to workshop drawings.

DÈLGHEM separates FEM node-to-node length, declared physical length and fabrication cut length. Geometrical operations are drawn only when the project explicitly supplies their position, dimensions and provenance.

Inside DÈLGHEM: models, connections and workshop

Three crops from authentic D2 app screenshots. Demo data only; drawings are not approved construction deliverables.

148 steel members and indexed drawings

The steel demo contains 148 members. Individual sheets and floor dossiers retain their identifiers. Missing fabrication data keep drawings explicitly partial rather than silently complete.

Holes, preparations, welds and stock

The Workshop editor accepts web/flange holes, end preparations, declared weld paths, tolerances and stock data. It does not infer weld resistance or turn FEM lengths into shop cutting lengths.

CAD scope and engineering limitations

The implemented workflow exports DXF and PDF. General assembly drawings, comprehensive executive design and structural verification retain open limitations.

Development status

DÈLGHEM vAlpha1 — planned release: January 2027. Scope subject to testing and change. This is not a finished, fully code-qualified professional release.
